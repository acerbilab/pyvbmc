At the shipped `tol_gp_noise = sqrt(1e-5)` and the level-0 hyperprior `student_t(log sqrt(1e-5), 0.5, 3)`, the added constant variance is of order 1e-5; in my reproduction at a deliberately large 0.01 the log-density difference is 8.2e-3. The upper half does not follow: at level 1 the total is `exp(2h₀) + exp(h₁)` and `h₀` runs up to `log(max y − min y)` on both sides, so the constant term can carry more (1070 in variance, SD 32.7, on the state measured).
