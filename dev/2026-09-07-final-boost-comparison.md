# Final-boost comparison: initial Phase 2 evidence

PI discussion update (2026-09-08): an acceptance rule can reject occasional
beneficial boosts without being unsuitable overall. The initial recommendation
below was too categorical. The decision is deferred: retain both full VPs and
their ELBO/SD, assess the magnitude and frequency of benefits and losses, and
select criteria post hoc. The PI proposed comparing penalty-on/off candidates
across the population; reconstruction of boost inputs from compact traces is
being checked before scheduling main-loop reruns.

Phase 2 started on `dev-final-boost`, based on validated `dev-next` commit
`3e879b6`. The [latent-bug plan](plans/latent-bug-fixes.md) owns execution
and the PI's decisions. The production tolerance remains open. The tested
rule catches the severe logistic failure but produces a clear posterior-shape
regression on noisy Rosenbrock seed 17, so neither tolerance is recommended
for production on this evidence. This note
separates the stored reference candidates from candidates generated by the
revised boost objective; both the seven-case check and two targeted follow-ups
are complete.

## Stored reference candidates

The scan covers all 870 reference runs, including the new 30-seed noisy
Rosenbrock D2 and Student D8 configurations. For each selected `best_iter`,
define `dE = final_elbo - pre_elbo` and `dS = final_sd - pre_sd`.
The proposed rule accepts when `min(dE, dE - 5*dS) > -tolerance`.
These candidates were generated with the **old boost weight penalty**.

| Tolerance | Rejected / 870 | New noisy Rosenbrock rejections | New noisy Student rejections |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.1 | 17 | 2 | 0 |
| 0.2 | 9 | 2 | 0 |

Neither threshold rejects any of the older 100 noisy runs. The two new
Rosenbrock cases are seeds 7 and 17; their worst score drops are 0.293490
and 6.564912 nats respectively, both at uncertainty weight 5.

Posterior diagnostics were reconstructed for 83 cases: every rejected
candidate, cases within 0.025 nats of either threshold, and all 60 new noisy
runs. They use the existing benchmark diagnostics and fixed independent
diagnostic generators. Every reconstructed final matched its sidecar's
evidence error, gsKL, MMTV, RMSE and moment method, and the stored posterior
mean/covariance, at `rtol=1e-9, atol=1e-11`. The final transformer is absent
from historical traces: reconstruction assumes the transform at `best_iter`.
Matching diagnostics validate this use; they do not certify missing state.

Both new noisy rejections would discard improvements in posterior shape:

| Rosenbrock noise SD 3 | Evidence error, pre → boost | gsKL, pre → boost | MMTV, pre → boost |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Seed 7 | 0.146627 → 0.011186 | 0.279226 → 0.265060 | 0.089803 → 0.072658 |
| Seed 17 | 0.311604 → 0.664783 | 6.401942 → 0.352013 | 0.318773 → 0.211659 |

Seed 7 improves all three metrics. Seed 17 improves posterior shape markedly
while evidence error increases. Neither tolerance distinguishes these cases.
Score rejection is therefore not synonymous with improving inference.

Across the rejected candidates, the number for which reverting would improve
each metric is:

| Tolerance | Evidence error | gsKL | MMTV |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.1 (17 rejections) | 14 | 13 | 14 |
| 0.2 (9 rejections) | 6 | 6 | 6 |

The additional eight rejections at 0.1 improve evidence error and MMTV in
all eight cases, and gsKL in seven. These counts weigh every case equally;
they do not quantify the magnitude of benefits or losses and are not
prospective rejection rates for the revised objective.
MMTV and bounded posterior moments are Monte Carlo diagnostics; small
differences here are descriptive and have not been tested for significance.

The unguarded old boost changes the usable fraction from 24/30 to 23/30
for new noisy Rosenbrock, and leaves it at 14/30 for new noisy Student,
using the existing joint accuracy criterion. It does not follow that
rejecting the two Rosenbrock score drops would recover that lost usable run.

Reproduction (single-threaded BLAS, original `.venv/Scripts/python.exe`):

```powershell
python dev/scripts/boost_comparison.py dev/scripts/runs/golden/reference_870_20260907 --metrics-tags selected --out dev/scripts/runs/latent_fixes/boost_20260907/stored_metrics_870.json
```

The JSON records all 870 score pairs, rejection and near-threshold IDs,
per-configuration counts, and the 83 paired diagnostics. No optimization
was run and no reference file was modified by this analysis.

## Revised objective: seven specified trajectory checks

All seven completed in 4.6 minutes. Every stored non-timer main-loop array
and initial design matched the reference exactly. Each trajectory generated
one zero-weight-penalty boost candidate, then evaluated both thresholds on
that candidate. Both tolerances made the same seven decisions:

| Case | Worst score change | Decision at 0.1 and 0.2 | Pre gsKL | Candidate gsKL |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Normal D5 seed 0 | -0.009619 | Accept | 0.001814 | 0.000104 |
| Banana D2 seed 0 | +0.011807 | Accept | 0.157741 | 0.149428 |
| Half-normal D2 seed 0 | +0.008387 | Accept | 0.000840 | 0.000303 |
| Cigar D4 seed 0 | +0.017871 | Accept | 0.005559 | 0.000400 |
| Rosenbrock D2 noise SD 1 seed 0 | +0.068364 | Accept | 0.211535 | 0.063520 |
| Logistic D5 seed 5 | -20.273163 | Reject | 0.068067 | 3.541406 |
| Student D4 seed 19 | +0.010892 | Accept | 0.017721 | 0.010333 |

The logistic candidate remains severely inaccurate after removing weight
shrinkage. Rejecting it returns the exact pre-boost posterior. Compared with
the old reference final, returned gsKL improves from 3.526090 to 0.068067,
MMTV from 0.320079 to 0.058863, and evidence error from 2.721091 to 0.175048.

The comparator retains **one accuracy-fence flag**: this returned logistic
MMTV is 0.001169 above its descriptive population fence of 0.057694.
That is an explained residual outlier after a large improvement, not a
new main-loop discrepancy. Independent review confirmed this interpretation;
the flag and original fence remain unchanged. The other six runs have no
accuracy-fence flags. Historical returned-transformer state remains
uncertifiable in the canonical comparison.

Raw candidates, exact pre-boost restart inputs, both decisions, diagnostics
and comparison reports are under
`dev/scripts/runs/latent_fixes/boost_20260907/revised_seven/captures/`;
the compact trajectory pairs are in its parent directory. The initial
driver is preserved as `boost_replay_initial.py` in the Phase 2 evidence
directory, SHA256 `87e5cd16fe09fa516a698471440222ecd8599797c915716e2ecd0d1fe432933b`.

The experimental branch
requires an explicit tolerance to activate zero boost weight penalty and
the continuum guard. `None` temporarily preserves legacy refinement;
it is not the intended final production default.

## Targeted noisy counterexamples

Rosenbrock D2 noise SD 3 seeds 7 and 17 are selected for follow-up because
both thresholds reject their old candidates despite improved posterior
shape. These tests diagnose those two counterexamples; selection based on
observed outcomes prevents using them to estimate rejection frequency or
calibrate a production tolerance. Both completed in 4.5 minutes with exact
main-loop and initial-design matches, and both thresholds rejected each
new candidate:

| Noise SD 3 case | Worst score change | Evidence error, pre → candidate | gsKL, pre → candidate | MMTV, pre → candidate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Seed 7 | -1.012730 | 0.146627 → 0.084229 | 0.279226 → 0.271840 | 0.089803 → 0.077778 |
| Seed 17 | -6.553628 | 0.311604 → 0.665581 | 6.401942 → 0.351809 | 0.318773 → 0.211992 |

Removing boost weight shrinkage does not resolve these counterexamples.
For seed 7, rejection discards improvements in all three diagnostics.
For seed 17, rejection improves evidence error but discards a large posterior
shape improvement. Its returned gsKL of 6.401942 exceeds the population
fence of 1.460436 and is much worse than the old reference final's 0.352013.
This second accuracy-fence flag is a **real adverse consequence of the tested
guard**, unlike the logistic case's residual fence exceedance after improvement.
Both flags remain in the reports.

Changing between tolerances 0.1 and 0.2 does not affect either case. In all
nine revised trajectories the two tolerances make identical decisions;
three candidates are rejected. These selected diagnostics establish concrete
benefits and counterexamples, not their population frequencies. Reconsider
the acceptance rule with the PI before choosing its default or promoting
this opt-in implementation. Q1 and the PyTorch decisions remain unchanged.

Artifacts are under
`dev/scripts/runs/latent_fixes/boost_20260907/noisy_counterexamples/`, with
restart state, raw candidates and reports in `captures/`. The driver retains
the original seven defaults; these two require explicit `--case` selections:

```powershell
python dev/scripts/boost_replay.py --out dev/scripts/runs/latent_fixes/boost_20260907/noisy_counterexamples --case rosenbrock_D2_noise3:7 --case rosenbrock_D2_noise3:17
```

Use a fresh output directory for a new experiment; existing files are never
overwritten. Recorded run times include diagnostic capture and metric work
and are not a controlled performance comparison.

## Verification and disposition

The 118 focused boost/options/initialization/analysis tests passed with
retries disabled. Eight exact oracle fixtures passed before and after the
change; the five pre-change replays matched all stored loop/final values.
The nine revised trajectories all retained exact main loops and initial
designs, generated one raw candidate each, and returned finite results.
The two accuracy-fence flags have distinct interpretations explained above.
Pinned Black 23.3.0 formatting checks passed for all changed Python files.
Independent Sol review checked the implementation, experiment provenance
and scientific reporting, with no remaining implementation/reporting findings.
All 1,740 reference files still match the published 870-run manifest.
The production default remains undecided; passing implementation checks
does not resolve the demonstrated scientific counterexample.
